Read a fragrance

Every scent has three movements.

01 — THE OPENING

Top notes

The first bright impression—usually citrus, spice or aromatic leaves. It introduces the mood.

02 — THE CHARACTER

Heart notes

The emotional centre, where flowers, herbs and warm spices begin to feel personal.

03 — THE MEMORY

Base notes

Woods, amber, musk and resins that settle into skin and stay after everything else softens.

How to test

Give it a full day.

Fragrance is chemistry, memory and weather. A blotter can only tell you the beginning.

Spray once on your wrist, let it dry naturally and notice it again after one hour, four hours and at the end of the day. The scent that keeps pulling you back is usually the one.
