Born in Chennai

Indian memory.

Buva turns the flowers, woods, coastlines and warm evenings of South India into a new fragrance language.

Our beginning

A perfume house shaped by place.

Chennai teaches you to notice scent: jasmine threaded at dawn, rain rising from stone, sandalwood in quiet rooms and salt carried in from the coast.

Buva was created to hold those moments without nostalgia. Our perfumes are direct, contemporary and designed for life now—made to sit beautifully on skin in heat, movement and changing light.
